What to bring

  • Swimsuit and a towel (we have wetsuits)
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and sunglasses with a strap
  • A warm layer for boat trips. Sunset gets chilly.
  • Water bottle and a snack
  • Whale watch? Take motion meds an hour before if you need them

Weather + whale promise

  • Change your date up to 48 hours out, free
Do I need to know how to swim?+

For kayaking and sailing, no. Everyone wears a life jacket. For snorkeling, you should be comfortable in the water, and we'll fit you with a wetsuit and a snorkel vest.

How old do kids need to be?+

Kids 6+ on kayak tours (in a tandem with an adult), 8+ for snorkeling, and all ages on boat trips.

Will I get seasick?+

Kayak and sunset trips stay close to shore and are usually calm. Whale watching goes offshore, so if you're prone to it, take motion meds an hour before and sit at the back of the boat.

Is it cold?+

The water runs about 58–72°F through the year. Wetsuits are included on paddle and snorkel tours, and we keep blankets on the boats.

Can we book a private trip?+

Yes. Private sunset charters work great for birthdays, proposals and team outings, up to 12 guests. Book it online or text us.
