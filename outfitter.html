Know before you go

Everything you need, in one spot.

We text this to you the day before, too. No digging through emails at the gas station.

Meeting spot

Hollow Pine Landing
Check in 30 minutes before your trip. Free parking right by the shuttle.

What to bring

  • Water shoes or sandals with straps (no flip-flops)
  • Sunscreen, hat and sunglasses with a strap
  • A full water bottle per person
  • Phone in a dry bag (rent one for $6)
  • Clothes you don't mind getting wet
  • Leave the glass at home. It's river rules.

Weather policy

Rain or shine, we float. Lightning, high water or storms in the forecast? We call it, not you.

  • If we cancel, you pick: free rebook or full refund
  • We text you by 7am the day of your trip
  • You can change your date up to 48 hours out, free
Do I need paddling experience?+

Nope. The river is gentle, about 2 mph, and our crew gives a quick lesson before you launch. Tubing needs zero skill at all.

How old do kids need to be?+

Kids 6 and up on day trips, with an adult in their boat or tube. Overnight trips are 12 and up. Kid-size life jackets are included.

Can I bring a cooler?+

Yes, soft coolers only, no glass. Or rent one of ours for $12 and we'll strap it to your boat.

Do you take big groups?+

All the time. Church groups, reunions, bachelor parties, scout troops. Groups of 15+ get a group rate and their own shuttle time.

Is there camping nearby?+

Our campground is right next to the landing, with tent sites, showers and a fire ring at every site. Add it when you book an overnight float.
