Questions

The ones I hear most.

Are you open to contract or project work?+

Always. Along with my own clients, I'm open to contract and project work with teams and agencies, whether that's web design, systems, brand or storytelling. Reach out and tell me what you need.

How much does a website cost?+

Landing pages are $1,000 flat. Custom sites start at $2,000. Systems, film and brand projects are quoted per project. Every project gets a fixed quote up front.

Do you do social, brand and video work?+

Yes, I do all three. Brand, social content and short films are quoted per project, since every one is different. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll send a clear quote.

Do you do Google Ads or SEO?+

I set up the basics every site needs: your Google Business Profile, page titles and a fast, phone-friendly site. I don't run ad campaigns. My focus is what happens after someone finds you.

How long does it take?+

Most sites go live in about 12 days. Bigger builds with lots of systems take longer, and I'll tell you up front.

What do I need to provide?+

Not much. A short call, your logo and photos if you have them, and quick feedback. I write the copy and handle the tech.

How does payment work?+

50% to start, 50% at launch. The Care Plan is billed monthly after launch.
